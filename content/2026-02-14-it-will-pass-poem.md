A poem a friend of mine recently shared:

> The Fear
>
> It softens when you breathe;
> through it, one moment at a time.
>
> The Overwhelm
>
> It settles as you settle down and come back to yourself.
>
> The Heaviness
>
> It lifts when you stop carrying it alone.
>
> The Anxiety
>
> It eases when you stop fighting and start soothing.
>
> The Doubt 
>
> It quietens as you remember who you truly are.
>
> The Sadness 
>
> It moves through you when you allow it space.
>
> The Discomfort
>
> It passes as you stop bracing and start softening.
> 
> It will pass...
> the uncertainty;
> the overwhelm;
> the heaviness;
> the fear;
> the struggle;
> the anxiety.
